Capabilities

your infrastructure

Most teams juggle five different tools to answer simple questions. Liminal replaces that entire stack.

Real-Time Fleet Visibility

Live cards for fleet size, availability, OS distribution, domain users, disk health, and activity trends. Filter by domain when you manage more than one environment.

CPU & RAM Disk Health Uptime 30+ Data Points

Vulnerability Intelligence

Continuously ingests CVEs from NVD and CIRCL feeds, matches them against installed software on your servers, and surfaces results ranked by CVSS severity.

CVE Matching CVSS Scoring NVD + CIRCL

Software Deployments

Push MSI, EXE, Winget, or ZIP packages to individual servers or broadcast fleet-wide. SHA256 verification on every download.

Alarms & Notifications

Server offline, disk critical, service crashed — get instant alerts on Discord, Teams, Slack, or email. Per-server maintenance windows.

Windows Update Management

See missing updates across your fleet, deploy them on your schedule, and track reboot status. No WSUS required.

Domain Intelligence

GPO trees with links, settings, and WMI filters. DNS zones with full record detail. AD user lists. All collected automatically.

Complete Audit Trail

Every action — deployments, logins, configuration changes, agent events — logged in a searchable Logbook with per-server drill-down.
